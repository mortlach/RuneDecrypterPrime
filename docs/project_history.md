# Project history

Rune Decrypter Prime began many years ago. I started developing tools, data and
techniques for working with the Cicada 3301 puzzles in 2014. Over the following
years that work moved through many different abstractions, tools, solvers, scorers,
experiments, public and private repositories before being brought together into RDP.

Much of that is still visible on the
[mortlach GitHub account](https://github.com/mortlach), together with all my surviving
solving activity around Cicada solvers. Private repositories and personal solving logs
cover parts of the development that were never public.

The implementations have changed repeatedly. The work on rune handling,
language data, ciphers, search methods and Liber Primus did not appear overnight.

RDP V1 is an attempt to bring that accumulated work into one package that other
people can install, inspect, test and extend.

Most of this work was done for fun and my own learning, but also with the aim of
building a robust community tool that helps people collaborate around a common
framework.

Modern AI coding tools became useful enough during 2025 that I began using them
in RDP development.

Used well, they have accelerated the work. In particular they made it
practical to expand implementation, testing, documentation, consistency checks
and robustness work much further than I could have managed at the same pace
alone.

They did not replace the earlier cryptanalytic work or its provenance. They
became another development tool used to implement, review and test it.

No matter the development workflow, the same standard still applies to the result:
code, examples and claims should be independently inspectable and verifiable
from the repository, the inputs and the evidence produced by the run.
