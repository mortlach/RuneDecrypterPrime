# CURRENT_RISKS.md

## Risk 001 — second-surface drift
Description:
A second public run/config surface appears during RunSpec convergence.

Mitigation:
- front-door inventory first
- ADR-0004 first
- guardrail tests before bridge growth

## Risk 002 — campaign simplification drift
Description:
Serious LP/no-WLI campaign behaviour gets flattened into prettier but weaker wrappers.

Mitigation:
- preserve current stage/policy substance on paper first
- use no-WLI as first façade consumer
- proof pass on flagship path

## Risk 003 — output/privacy fragmentation persists
Description:
Old local writers or path logic survive and undermine the shared contract.

Mitigation:
- inventory every current writer/policy owner
- define shared contract before code movement
- keep path/privacy tests visible

## Risk 004 — report/reconstruction under-specification
Description:
ScorerReport/SolverReport/rescoring state remains too vague, leading to drift.

Mitigation:
- freeze minimum report contracts early
- add reconstruction tests before campaign lift

## Risk 005 — planning pack becomes stale or ornamental
Description:
Agents stop reading it, or it stops tracking real work.

Mitigation:
- require read-first order
- keep ACTIVE_TODO small and reviewed
- tie each slice to rules, tests, and files
